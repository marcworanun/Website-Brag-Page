# Embracing Eczema logo

Marc chose **option 2** (17 Oct 2026); it's on the website as `images/embracing-eczema-logo.png`.

Two options, both built from Marc's photos of the Embracing Eczema handover sign and the Embrace Gloves:

- **Option 1**: the heart-and-rays mark from the existing sign, rising over three waves in the glove colours (pink, lilac, blue).
- **Option 2**: a pink glove and a blue glove meeting to form a heart, dotted with the gloves' ventilation holes.

Files for each option: `stacked` (icon above the name), `horizontal` (icon beside the name), `icon` (mark only, for profile pictures). PNGs have transparent backgrounds and are rendered at 4x; the `icon` SVGs scale to any size. `logo-options-preview.png` shows both options on white, on the site's green and at small sizes.

Colours: heart #b23a5b, pink #ee8fab, lilac #a992d6, blue #6fb3e3, text #3b4048. Font: Poppins Medium, letter-spacing 0.32em.

`logos-source.html` is the source: open it in a browser to see every version, or edit it and re-render.
