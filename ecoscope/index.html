Who we are

We are a fashion organisation weaving sustainable textiles composed of sustainable materials, adorned with modern Thai designs.

We cultivate wasted fruits and their trees around Thailand to form our threads and dyes. Our framework creates a system where profits are created from the decomposition and up-cycling of harvest.

Through our clothes we aim to forge a greener and more educated society around fashion embedded with Thai culture.

Why it matters

The waste already exists. We treat it as a material, not a problem.

1.3M

Tonnes of bananas produced in Thailand each year — and the trees left behind.

40%

Of all food waste is fruit, much of it unsold for looking imperfect.

Once

A banana tree fruits a single time in its life, then is usually burned.

100%

Of profit goes to the artisans who weave our clothes and to animal conservation groups.

The material

Banana stem fibre

Banana trees fruit once, then take up space until they are cleared — most often by burning. We take the stems instead: short-rooted, low-thirst, naturally dyed, biodegradable, and far more durable than they look.

  • Low water consumption
  • Biodegradable
  • Prevents the cremation of banana trees
  • Naturally dyed & highly durable

The makers

Jaeson village

Our clothes are made by the Jaeson village community, on traditional wooden looms. Our profits go to these artisans and to animal conservation groups such as the Save Elephant Foundation.

On the 10th of July we travelled there to understand the process first hand — and to say thank you in person.
