Pop-ups

We take Ecoscope to markets, parks, hotels and festivals, so people can feel the cloth, meet our team, and hear the stories behind each design.

Our clothes are made to be seen in everyday places, not only online. Every pop-up is a chance to show where our fibre comes from, and to talk about the endangered animals in our Wild Threads collection.
