Wild Threads

Thailand’s forests, rivers and seas are home to animals that are quietly disappearing. Each piece in this collection is made for one of them.

Clothes are seen every day, by everyone who passes the person wearing them. We use that to speak for the animals Thailand is losing. Every piece in Wild Threads is designed around a single endangered species, so wearing it carries that animal’s story out into the street.

Below, each animal sits beside the piece made for it, with what makes it remarkable, why it is in danger, and its status on the IUCN Red List — the global record of how close a species is to extinction.
