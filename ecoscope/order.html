The collection

Four pieces woven in Jaeson village from banana fibre, plus the Pangolin Wallet, all made in small runs.

Choose a size or colour, add what you want to your bag, and send it to us in one message. We confirm stock and delivery with you directly.

Equus Long Sleeve

1,500 ฿

Waffle-knit long sleeve in undyed white, printed with a watercolour of horses mid-gallop. The open waffle texture breaks the ink along its edges, so the print reads as painted into the cloth rather than pressed onto it. Ribbed crew neck and cuffs, straight through the body.

Banana stem fibre · naturally dyed · handwoven in Jaeson village

Jaeson Hoodie

1,500 ฿

A zip-through hood panelled in two weights of the village’s striped cloth — the lighter stripe across the chest, shoulders and hood, the darker set into curved panels that sweep from the underarm down each sleeve. Full-length zip, unlined, with gathered cuffs and hem. Light enough for the rainy season and easy to layer in the cool months.

Banana stem fibre · naturally dyed · handwoven in Jaeson village

Jaeson T Shirt

1,500 ฿

Short-sleeve and button-through in a fine indigo stripe, with the collar cut from the lighter weave so it reads clearly against the body. Five buttons, and the Ecoscope mark embroidered at the lower left hem. Boxy through the chest with a side vent, so it hangs flat worn open or closed.

Banana stem fibre · naturally dyed · handwoven in Jaeson village

Jaeson Pants

1,500 ฿

Wide-leg trousers in the same handwoven check, with crescent panels set into each knee in the lighter cloth. Belt loops, a zip fly and five pockets, cut with a straight fall from hip to hem that lets the weave keep its drape.

Banana stem fibre · naturally dyed · handwoven in Jaeson village

Pangolin Wallet

500 ฿

A bifold wallet whose textured cover is layered like a pangolin’s scales, made for the Sunda pangolin in our Wild Threads collection. Card slots inside, with the Ecoscope name on the inner panel.

Grey or yellow · part of Wild Threads
