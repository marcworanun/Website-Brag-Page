About us

We are a fashion organisation weaving sustainable textiles composed of sustainable materials, adorned with modern Thai designs.

We cultivate wasted fruits and their trees across Thailand to form our threads and dyes. Our framework creates a system where profits are created from the decomposition and up-cycling of harvest.

Through our clothes we aim to forge a greener and more educated society around fashion embedded with Thai culture.

The problems

01Material

Unsustainable fashion

Widely used materials such as cotton and polyester over-consume water and are non-biodegradable. Organic cotton, and other materials that are variations of their original form, surprisingly still bear similar harmful effects.

02Waste

Fruit & fruit tree waste

Fruit sellers do not sell their unappealing produce, so fruit makes up 40% of all food waste combined — a prevalent issue in a fruit-rich country like Thailand. Many fruit trees, banana trees in particular, produce fruit only once per lifetime. They consume mass amounts of space and so are cremated in most regions, contributing to global warming.

03Inequality

Poverty

There are many underfunded manufacturers in rural regions of Thailand whose brilliant ideas are often left unknown.

Our initiatives

Education, and clothes that argue for themselves.

Education

We educate on materials that are sustainable alternatives composed of fruit, whilst using them to produce our clothes as examples to the industry.

Clothing brand

We gather wasted fruit and their trees from farms and markets to make our clothes. Fruit fibres are often biodegradable and sturdy, while consuming little water. Many of our clothes are made from fruit trees that have been cut down and that otherwise would have been cremated.

We aim to find underprivileged manufacturers, as profits made are directly sent to their community.
