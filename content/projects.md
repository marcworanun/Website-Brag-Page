## 1. LunarLift

### Full notes

- **What it is:** a mobility rehabilitation device with a body weight support system and a safety system that protects the patient if they fall.
- **Why I started it:** my grandfather had a stroke and has difficulty walking. LunarLift was built to help him.
- **The problem:**
  - Rehab devices with body weight support are mostly found in rehab centres, often hours away from the countryside.
  - Those devices aren't built for home-based recovery, which makes consistent rehab difficult.
  - Devices that do support home-based recovery cost at least **5,500 USD**.
- **My solution:** LunarLift includes those features for **650 USD**, about 88% cheaper.
- **Testing:** trialled at **3 rural hospitals** with **20 real patients**.
- **Licensing:** worked on a licence agreement with the company **Retail Business Solutions**. As part of this deal I:
  - researched FDA registration pathways
  - compared LunarLift with other devices on the market
- **Future:** the licence agreement will be used to distribute the device to hospitals.
