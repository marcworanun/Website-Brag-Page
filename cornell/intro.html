Everything I do comes back to one goal: making wellbeing more affordable and sustainable, from hospital wards to hotel dining rooms.

I am a senior at Shrewsbury International School Bangkok, a researcher, a sustainable clothing brand owner and a budding economist.

I design healthcare products that work as well as what's on the market for a fraction of the price, make them from waste where I can, and use economics to help communities earn more. At two Bangkok hotels, I brought the same idea to hospitality: a wellness programme and healthier dining. Here is what I've built so far. Enjoy!
