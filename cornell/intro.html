Everything I do comes back to one goal: making wellbeing more affordable and sustainable, from hospital wards to hotel dining rooms.

I am a senior at Shrewsbury International School Bangkok, an aspiring researcher, a sustainable clothing brand owner, an environmentalist and a budding economist, and I'm still adding to that list...

I design healthcare products that work as well as what's on the market for a fraction of the price, I make them from waste wherever I can, and I use economics to help communities earn more from what they make. At two Bangkok hotels, I brought the same idea to hospitality, piloting a wellness and longevity programme and making healthy dishes a bigger share of what guests order. This page walks you through what I've built so far. Enjoy!
